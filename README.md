Vision-Based Precision Pose Estimation for Autonomous Formation Flying

Reimplementation of [Punnoose, Stanford CS229 (2020)](https://cs229.stanford.edu/proj2020spr/report/Punnoose.pdf).

**Team 9:** PES2UG24AM005, PES2UG24AM055

A follower aircraft observes 14 known points on a leader aircraft. Its relative pose is estimated in two stages:

1. **ANN:** maps 42 image features to one of 4800 coarse pose classes.
2. **Particle Filter:** refines the ANN estimate using dynamics and image observations.

All data is synthetic and generated by our simulator.

## Pose Representation

The pose is:

```text
[X, Y, Z, Roll]
```

The pose space is discretized into:

```text
X = 10 bins
Y = 10 bins
Z = 8 bins
Roll = 6 bins
```

Therefore:

```text
10 × 10 × 8 × 6 = 4800 classes
```

The ANN predicts one of these coarse pose classes.

# Part 2: Particle Filter

The Particle Filter refines the ANN's coarse pose.

Implementation:

```text
src/particle_filter.py
```

### 1. Initialize

```text
ANN coarse pose + sampled [ΔX, ΔY, ΔZ, ΔRoll]
                 ↓
          1000 / 5000 particles
```

Errors are sampled from:

```text
results/error_distribution.npz
```

### 2. Propagate

Particles are moved using the simulated flight dynamics in:

```text
src/simulator.py
```

### 3. Weight

Each candidate 3D pose is projected back to 2D:

```text
candidate 3D pose
       ↓
simulator.project()
       ↓
predicted 2D points
```

Predicted points are compared with observed points. Occluded points are ignored.

```text
weight = 1 / (MSE + 1e-6)
```

### 4. Resample

High-weight particles are favored, while fresh particles are added around the ANN estimate.

### 5. Estimate

The final PF estimate is the weighted average:

```text
refined [X, Y, Z, Roll]
```

# Implementation Differences

Compared with the original paper:

- Feature detection is skipped; the simulator directly provides the 14 points.
- A simplified 14-point aircraft model is used instead of full ray-traced rendering.
- Direct pinhole projection and simplified occlusion are used.
- Observation noise is included.
- Flight dynamics are simplified.
- The ANN uses `sklearn`'s `MLPClassifier`.
